About CurisNow Inc.

CurisNow Inc. was established with a singular mission: to redefine the way healthcare is accessed and paid for in the United States. As healthcare costs continue to rise, with projections indicating that spending will reach $6.0 trillion by 2027, many Americans are left struggling with high out-of-pocket expenses and a complex, opaque payment system. At CurisNow, we believe that quality healthcare should be both accessible and affordable for everyone, regardless of insurance status.

For Patients

Our platform is designed to empower patients by providing them with a transparent and user-friendly marketplace. Through CurisNow, patients can easily search for, compare, and book appointments with healthcare providers across various specialties, all based on clear, upfront pricing. Whether you’re seeking a routine check-up, dental care, or physical therapy, CurisNow connects you with providers who offer the services you need at prices you can afford. We cater especially to the uninsured and underinsured, a demographic that often faces the highest financial barriers to care.

For Providers

For healthcare providers, CurisNow offers a unique opportunity to reach a broader patient base, particularly those who are proactive about managing their health and seeking cost-effective care. Our platform allows providers to focus on what they do best—delivering high-quality healthcare—while CurisNow takes care of the rest, including ensuring prompt payments and facilitating patient appointments. By reducing administrative burdens and promoting price transparency, we help providers grow their practices and improve patient satisfaction.

Our Goal

CurisNow does not manage or store patient health data, ensuring that the relationship between patients and providers remains direct and secure. Our goal is to create an open market that encourages competition, reduces costs, and enhances the overall patient experience.

At CurisNow Inc., we are not just another healthcare platform—we are a catalyst for change in the healthcare industry. By breaking down the barriers to care, we are making quality healthcare more accessible, more affordable, and more transparent for everyone.
