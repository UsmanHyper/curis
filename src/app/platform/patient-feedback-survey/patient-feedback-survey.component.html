Notice of Privacy Practices

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY

OVERVIEW

This notice describes how CurisNow Inc. (“Marketplace,” “we,” or “us”) may disclose health information about you, your rights regarding that information, and our legal obligations to protect it. Curis is a healthcare marketplace connecting users with providers, sharing necessary information to carry out treatment, payment, and healthcare operations. All entities associated with Curis adhere to the terms of this notice.

We are legally required to ensure your health information is kept private, notify you following any breach of your unsecured protected health information, and follow the terms outlined here. You may request a paper copy of this notice, and we reserve the right to change our privacy practices and this notice at any time.

HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

We may use and disclose your health information for providing services, including treatment, payment, and healthcare operations. For example:

  • Treatment: Sharing your health information with providers within the Curis network for consultations or referrals.
  • Payment: Submitting claims to your insurance company for reimbursement.
  • Healthcare Operations: Reviewing treatment procedures for quality assurance and compliance.

Written authorization is required for uses beyond these purposes, except under specific circumstances outlined below.

DISCLOSURES WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION

We may disclose your health information without authorization in the following situations:

  • Emergencies: To address immediate health emergencies
  • Judicial and Administrative Proceedings: In response to valid legal processes.
  • Public Health and Safety: If you are an immediate danger to yourself or others, or in cases of abuse or neglect.
  • Health Oversight Activities: For government audits or inspections.
  • Business Associates: To partners performing services on our behalf, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Research: Under specific circumstances and typically with non-identifying information.
  • Marketing and Communication: To inform you about services or schedule appointments, unless you opt out.

YOUR INDIVIDUAL RIGHTS

You have rights regarding your health information, including:

  • Right to Inspect and Copy: Access your health information with certain exceptions.
  • Right to Amend: Request corrections to your health information.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: Receive a list of non-routine disclosures of your information.
  • Right to Request Restrictions: Limit how your information is used or disclosed.
  • Right to Request Confidential Communications: Specify how and where you wish to be contacted about your health care.
  • Right to File Complaints: Contact us or the Secretary of Health and Human Services if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

EMAIL AND TEXT MESSAGES

Communications via email or text may not be secure. For urgent matters, please contact emergency services directly.
